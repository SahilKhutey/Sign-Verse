# SignVerse AI: Innovation & Expansion Roadmap (2024)

This document outlines the strategic research and business development goals for SignVerse AI through the remainder of 2024.

## 1. Research Roadmap

### Q2 2024: Cross-lingual & Co-speech
- **Cross-lingual sign translation**: Support for ASL ↔ ISL ↔ BSL translation.
- **Co-speech gesture generation**: Generative models that take text + emotion as input to output synchronized co-speech gestures.

### Q3 2024: Digital Avatars & Bidirectional Communication
- **Sign language avatar generation**: High-fidelity 3D/generative avatars for sign output.
- **Real-time bidirectional communication**: Fully integrated speech-to-sign and sign-to-speech loops for interactive dialogue.

### Q4 2024: Edge & Immersive
- **Mobile edge deployment**: Lightweight model architectures optimized for on-device inference (Android/iOS).
- **AR/VR integration**: Immersive signing experiences using headset-mounted sensors.

## 2. Business Development

### Partnerships
- **Educational institutions**: Collaboration with Gallaudet University and others for dataset refinement and user testing.
- **Accessibility organizations**: Integration with NGOs serving the d/Deaf and hard-of-hearing communities.
- **Government agencies**: Alignment with ADA compliance standards for public services.

### Monetization
- **Enterprise API licensing**: High-throughput inference for healthcare, legal, and educational platforms.
- **Educational platform subscriptions**: Specialized tools for sign language learners.
- **Government/Healthcare contracts**: Specialized deployments for accessibility-critical sectors.

### Open Source Strategy
- **Research Transparency**: Release of foundation models under specialized research licenses to accelerate academic progress.
- **Commercial API**: Maintain state-of-the-art managed APIs for production and enterprise use.
